# RC216 — RiverSSAI Simple Intelligence Dashboard

This PR adds a beginner-friendly Italian standalone HTML dashboard prototype.

- Plain-language navigation: overview, public-source signals, company watchlist, report, people/access simulation, and guide.
- Sample signals are clearly labeled as fictitious.
- Watchlist and people invitations are browser-only demonstration interactions, not persisted or sent.
- Privacy-first design goal: personal profile and explicit user-controlled invitations.
- No real login, access-control system, source ingestion, notifications, financial accounts, or advice/execution is implemented.
- A production version must implement secure authentication, invitation flows, role-based permissions, audit logging, privacy and compliance review.

No merge or deployment.
