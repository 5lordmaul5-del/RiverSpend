# RC-176 — RiverSpend & RiverSSAI: Multidisciplinary Divisions Registry

Status: proposal/organizational catalog only. This document defines areas of knowledge and work intake—not staffed departments, credentials, deployed capability or authorization.

## Purpose
Create a shared directory of civilian, scientific, engineering, tactical/military-domain (non-operational), aerospace, robotics, mathematical, quantum, financial and enterprise expertise that RiverSpend and RiverSSAI can organize, route and coordinate. Human expertise and approval remain necessary.

## Organizational design
Each division has: an accountable human lead (when staffed), scope, specialist roles, competency requirements, projects, risk level, budget, deliverables, review gates, dependencies, evidence and learning records.

## Divisions
1. **Systems Architecture & Integration** — requirements, interfaces, system-of-systems design, lifecycle, integration and trade studies.
2. **Civil Engineering & Infrastructure** — buildings, transport infrastructure, structural design, urban systems, accessibility, maintenance and sustainability. Physical design/implementation requires qualified licensed professionals and local approval.
3. **Mechanical & Manufacturing Engineering** — mechanical systems, CAD, manufacturing process design, industrial automation, quality and supply-chain engineering.
4. **Electrical, Electronics & Embedded Systems** — circuits, power systems, electronics, embedded computing, instrumentation and hardware validation.
5. **Robotics & Mechatronics** — robotic systems, sensing, control, simulation, safe test environments, human-machine interaction and maintenance.
6. **Aerospace & Space Engineering** — aerospace systems research, propulsion/flight concepts, spacecraft systems, orbital mechanics study, simulation, reliability and compliance. High-risk practical work is gated and qualified.
7. **Aviation & Flight Systems** — aircraft systems, avionics, human factors, simulation, safety, maintenance engineering and regulatory compliance.
8. **Tactical & Military-Domain Studies** — organizational/logistical analysis, history, policy, ethics, procurement governance, resilience, safety, non-operational simulations and civilian oversight. No autonomous operational targeting, weapon construction, tactical engagement plans or harm-enabling instructions.
9. **Mathematics & Applied Science** — pure/applied mathematics, numerical analysis, optimization, statistics, probability, modeling, scientific computing and verification.
10. **Quantum Science & Technology** — quantum mechanics education/research, quantum computing algorithms at conceptual/simulation level, quantum information, sensing research and error-correction study; claims must be evidence-based.
11. **AI & Computational Engineering** — machine learning, scientific AI, simulation, model evaluation, data engineering, responsible AI and human oversight.
12. **Software & Platform Engineering** — architecture, application development, APIs, CI/CD, test engineering, documentation and maintainability.
13. **Cloud, Data & Infrastructure** — compute, storage, observability, resilience, portability, databases, data governance and cost control.
14. **Cybersecurity, Privacy & Digital Resilience** — defensive security, threat modeling, secure development, privacy, recovery, incident management and audit.
15. **Telecommunications & Networking** — network design, protocols, communications infrastructure, reliability and lawful security research.
16. **Audio, Acoustics & Immersive Media** — audio systems, DSP, acoustics, sound design, accessibility and spatial audio.
17. **Video, Imaging, Cinema & Streaming** — imaging pipelines, production tools, encoding, streaming systems, computer vision with privacy safeguards.
18. **Games & Interactive Engineering** — game engines, simulation, graphics, tools, interaction design, testing and accessible experiences.
19. **Materials & Product Design** — materials research, ergonomics, industrial design, sustainable materials and manufacturing readiness.
20. **Energy & Environmental Engineering** — energy systems, efficiency, renewables, environmental modeling, lifecycle analysis and sustainability.
21. **Financial Engineering & Risk** — quantitative finance, pricing research, risk measurement, forecasting, portfolio theory education, financial systems and model validation. No guaranteed returns or personalized regulated advice.
22. **Economics & Business Research** — macro/microeconomics, market analysis, economic modeling, policy, competition and business research.
23. **Corporate Strategy & Enterprise Operations** — company structure, operating models, governance, project portfolios, process design, KPIs and decision support.
24. **Accounting, Treasury & Financial Operations** — bookkeeping concepts, cash-flow planning, budgets, cost accounting, controls, reconciliation and compliant reporting; professional validation required.
25. **Legal, Compliance & Standards** — legal research, privacy, intellectual property, consumer rights, contracts, export controls, standards mapping and compliance evidence; qualified counsel validates.
26. **Supply Chain, Logistics & Procurement** — supplier evaluation, traceability, logistics modeling, procurement governance, inventory and continuity.
27. **Quality, Testing, Reliability & Certification Support** — test planning, verification, validation, reliability, documentation, defect management and certification readiness.
28. **Research, Education & Knowledge Management** — research methods, literature reviews, training, competency development, technical library and institutional knowledge.
29. **Human Factors, Accessibility & Safety** — ergonomics, accessibility, user research, safety culture, usability and wellbeing.
30. **Project, Program & Portfolio Management** — planning, dependencies, risk, scope, budgets, milestones, reporting and stakeholder coordination.

## RiverSSAI coordination role
RiverSSAI may assist with:
- Classifying requests and recommending relevant division(s).
- Drafting project charters, requirements and checklists.
- Creating research summaries, risk registers and dependency maps.
- Coordinating approved task queues and documenting decisions.
- Preparing test plans and evidence templates.
- Maintaining a knowledge catalog with source, date, confidence and reviewer.
- Flagging specialist, legal, safety, financial, or regulated review requirements.

RiverSSAI does not claim credentials, replace qualified professionals, independently approve safety-critical work, or execute unapproved consequential actions.

## Standard project lifecycle
1. Intake and classification
2. Purpose, stakeholder and feasibility review
3. Requirements, budget and risks
4. Design/research proposal
5. Human authorization for any consequential step
6. Prototype, simulation or research work
7. Test, verification and independent review as risk demands
8. Documentation, decision and learning record
9. Release or handoff only after explicit authorization

## Shared project record
Required fields: project ID, title, requester, owner, division tags, purpose, scope, deliverables, budget estimate, schedule, dependencies, risk tier, applicable law/standards, data handling, approvals, test evidence, decision log and status.

## Risk tiers
- **Tier 0 — Learning/research:** educational summaries, basic analysis and harmless simulations.
- **Tier 1 — Low-risk digital prototype:** controlled test data and sandboxed environment.
- **Tier 2 — Real-world systems:** engineering review, safety and privacy assessment.
- **Tier 3 — Safety-critical or regulated:** licensed/qualified experts, independent validation, compliance review, controlled facilities and formal approvals.
- **Restricted domains:** work involving weapons, operational military planning, targeting, destructive cyber activity or other harm-enabling tasks is outside this catalog's permitted scope; route only to safe, high-level, defensive, historical, governance or policy analysis.

## Principles
Human dignity; transparent budgets; qualified supervision; responsible science; safety first; privacy; accessibility; lawful work; no false claims; traceable evidence; stop-work authority; independent review where risk warrants.

## Suggested implementation sequence
Phase A: add this registry and schemas to RiverSSAI's planning/coordination layer.
Phase B: create division cards, project intake and a transparent dashboard.
Phase C: prioritize digital, civilian and simulation projects with measurable tests.
Phase D: build partnerships and recruitment plans as resources permit.
Phase E: consider specialized physical facilities only after funding, staffing, permits, insurance, safety and independent review.
