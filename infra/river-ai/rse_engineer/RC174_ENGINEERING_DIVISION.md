# RSEngineer — Multidisciplinary Engineering & Research Division (RC-174)

RSEngineer is the proposed multidisciplinary engineering, research, design, prototyping and recruiting organization of RiverSpend. It is a catalog and organizational blueprint, not a claim that staff have been hired or facilities exist.

## Engineering divisions

- Aerospace & Space Systems: spacecraft concepts, satellites, avionics, orbital systems, propulsion research, mission systems, safety and simulation.
- Robotics & Automation: industrial/service robots, autonomous systems, control, sensing, mechatronics and robotics safety.
- Aviation & Flight Systems: aircraft systems, UAV/drone engineering, flight control, navigation, safety, aerodynamics and simulation.
- Sound & Acoustics: audio engineering, DSP, studio design, immersive audio, acoustics and sound hardware.
- Video, Imaging & Cinema: camera systems, codecs, image processing, editing/postproduction, visual effects and video infrastructure.
- Game Engineering: game engines, gameplay systems, graphics, multiplayer, simulation, tools and accessibility.
- Software & Systems Engineering: architecture, apps, backend, APIs, operating systems, testing, DevOps and reliability.
- AI & Computational Engineering: ML systems, inference infrastructure, robotics intelligence, simulation, evaluation and responsible AI.
- Hardware & Electronics: PCB, embedded systems, firmware, sensors, power electronics, prototyping and product validation.
- Computer & Infrastructure Engineering: computing devices, servers, networking, storage, cloud/local infrastructure and performance.
- Telecommunications & Networks: wireless, networking protocols, communications systems, signal processing and resilience.
- Materials & Industrial Design: materials research, CAD, manufacturing processes, ergonomics, product design and prototyping.
- Energy & Environmental Engineering: energy systems, power efficiency, thermal management, environmental monitoring and sustainability.
- Cybersecurity & Safety Engineering: threat modeling, secure design, safety cases, audits and risk management; high-risk systems require qualified independent review.
- Testing, Quality & Certification: validation, test rigs, reliability, interoperability, standards research, documentation and certification support.
- Research & Experimental Lab: cross-disciplinary exploration, feasibility studies, prototypes, technical literature and experiments.

## Recruiting and specialist role registry

Roles are to be recruited/contracted as real people or qualified external teams when resources and legal arrangements permit. No fictional staff or completed recruitment is implied.

Potential role families include: aerospace engineers; astronautics/mission systems engineers; avionics and flight engineers; robotics/mechatronics/control engineers; sound engineers and acousticians; video/image processing engineers; game developers and graphics engineers; software architects and developers; AI/ML and computational engineers; electronics/PCB/embedded/firmware engineers; computer and network engineers; telecom engineers; materials/manufacturing/industrial design engineers; energy/environmental engineers; security engineers; test/reliability/quality engineers; researchers, technicians, project managers and safety specialists.

## Work process

1. Intake and define requirements, budget, scope and risk.
2. Feasibility and research, with specialist consultation.
3. Design review and alternatives.
4. Prototype plan and resource estimate.
5. Build only in authorized and adequately resourced environments.
6. Test, document, assess risks and seek qualified reviews/certifications.
7. Human authorization for procurement, external actions, deployment and safety-critical work.

## Responsible boundaries

This registry does not create employees, physical laboratories, funding, credentials or capabilities. Aviation, aerospace, medical, industrial, weapons-adjacent, high-energy and other safety-critical systems require appropriate legal compliance, qualified professionals, secure facilities, independent safety reviews and authorization. No autonomous real-world operation is implied.
