# RiverFortress backup policy

- Maintain at least three copies of important data, across two storage types/providers, with one offline or immutable copy where feasible.
- Encrypt backups in transit and at rest.
- Use separate backup credentials and least-privilege access.
- Keep documented retention schedules and legal holds.
- Back up configuration, application data, database data, deployment artifacts and critical documentation.
- Verify integrity and perform restore drills on a schedule.
- Record restore results, duration, data loss window and corrective actions.
- Protect backups from routine production credentials and automated deletion.
- Define Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) before promising continuity.
