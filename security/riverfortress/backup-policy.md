# Backup policy

- Maintain multiple copies across separate storage systems/providers, including an offline or immutable copy where feasible.
- Encrypt backups at rest and in transit.
- Use separate credentials and least privilege.
- Keep retention schedules and legal holds documented.
- Back up data, configurations, deployment artifacts and critical documentation.
- Test integrity and restore regularly; record duration and data-loss window.
- Protect backups from production credentials and routine automated deletion.
- Define realistic RTO and RPO before making availability promises.
