# RiverPay — provider selection checklist

Select a PSP with marketplace/platform capabilities and a suitable contract for Italy/EU. Evaluate:
- Platform/marketplace account onboarding and seller KYC/AML responsibility boundaries.
- Provider-managed settlement and payout options; whether payout timing can be tied to fulfillment.
- Hosted checkout, payment methods, refunds, disputes, webhook signing, idempotency.
- Fees, reserves, chargebacks, support, countries/currencies, data processing and PCI scope.
- Contractual and regulatory status for the intended flow; obtain professional legal/accounting advice.

Potential candidates for evaluation (not endorsements or confirmed availability): Stripe Connect, Mangopay, PayPal Commerce Platform. RiverPay must not create its own stored-value wallet or hold customer funds without appropriate authorization and arrangements.
