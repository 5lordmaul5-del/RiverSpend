# RiverBand / VirtualSoundStudio: Creative release pipeline

A staged workflow for creators finishing songs, albums, soundtracks, podcasts, or video in RiverSpend VirtualSoundStudio / ISSA.

## Flow
1. Export/finish the project; prepare mix, master, stems or alternate versions where feasible.
2. Create a release record: title, artist, contributors, credits, track list, language, genre, date, artwork and version.
3. Enter identifiers such as ISRC per recording and UPC/EAN per release only if actually assigned; never invent identifiers.
4. Rights checklist: composition and master rights, co-writers, publisher, producer, performers, samples, interpolations, covers, artwork, territories/terms and evidence.
5. Review metadata and rights evidence; unresolved issues block readiness.
6. Prepare delivery package and manifest; connect an authorized distributor only after its API, credentials, contracts and consent are configured.
7. Track distribution, withdrawal and links. Never claim registration/deposit/licensing/distribution without confirmation from the relevant service.
8. RiverSpendShop may sell authorized physical CDs or digital downloads only after rights, fulfilment, consumer, tax and payment readiness.

## SIAE / Soundreef
This workflow guides creators to official procedures but is not itself registration, membership, repertoire declaration, authorization or legal advice. SIAE and Soundreef are distinct services; check eligibility, fees, mandates, repertoire management, exclusivity and terms. Covers, samples, adaptations and artwork require appropriate clearance. Store receipts and confirmations. No automatic submission is claimed.

## Status flow
Draft -> metadata review -> rights review -> ready for distribution -> published; withdrawal is separately managed. Review and provider-confirmation gates are explicit. Unknown rights remain draft.

## Parallel ecosystem tracks
- RiverSSAI: assisted metadata/checklists and consistency suggestions, never legal certification.
- RiverCloud: secure project versioning and private evidence vault, with encryption and retention controls.
- VirtualSoundStudio: export assets, metadata and handoff package.
- ISSA: preview/listening experience, not rights clearance.
- RiverSpendShop: listings, downloads, orders and fulfilment after readiness.

No external submission, payment or deployment is implied. Integrations require security/privacy, authentication, provider and legal review, and acceptance tests.
