# RC230 — RiverCine Studios Flagship Blueprint
## Mission
Build an original, integrated studio platform spanning film, television/series, animation, documentary, music video and game cinematics. Long-term aspiration: world-class capabilities, not a claim of present equivalence with Lucasfilm, Sony or Universal.

## Studio divisions
- RiverScript: concept, script, revision/versioning, scene breakdown.
- RiverStory: storyboard, shot list, animatic, blocking.
- RiverSAnimationS: character, rig, motion, animation.
- RiverVirtual Production: scene/virtual set, previs, camera, lighting.
- RiverVFX: compositing, tracking, FX and simulation workflows.
- RiverEdit: video editing, timeline, assembly and review.
- RiverColor: grading/look development.
- River ISSA: sound design, Foley, music, spatial audio.
- RiverSSAI Creative Intelligence: opt-in assistant for planning, continuity, checks and suggestions.
- RiverRender: render queues, quality presets and asset validation.
- RiverGames Cinematics: game/cinematic bridge.
- RiverDistribution: delivery formats, captions, metadata and rights checklist.

## Prototype in this PR
Production-project form, pipeline checklist and JSON export. All studio divisions are roadmap cards.

## Development stages
1. Studio project database and asset registry.
2. Script editor and breakdown.
3. Storyboard canvas and animatic.
4. Timeline/editing foundation.
5. Animation and character rig.
6. VFX/compositing foundation.
7. Audio integration and mix workflow.
8. Render orchestration and QC.
9. Collaboration, permissions, review and versioning.
10. Distribution packaging.

## Safety / rights
Maintain asset licensing and provenance, talent permissions, privacy, human review, safe content practices and security. Do not imitate protected characters or proprietary software.
## Limitations
This is a workflow blueprint/prototype, not a film-grade production suite or production infrastructure. No tests run.
