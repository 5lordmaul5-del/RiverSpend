# RC259 — RiverSSAI Motif, Harmony & Form Engine

A browser-side procedural experiment generates a seed motif, a simple degree-based chord sequence and a four-stage form plan from user-selected key, mode, mood, BPM and bar count. It provides a basic synthesized playback and JSON export.

## Scope and limitations
- Procedural, deterministic pattern sets; not an AI model.
- Simplified pitch/motif and harmony; no professional voice-leading, rhythm notation, counterpoint, transposition/range checks, full-score creation or audio production.
- Playback uses a browser oscillator, not a soundfont or orchestral library.
- No external API, backend, or integration with RiverSSAI runtime.

## Future work
Implement music-theory validation, chord voicing, rhythmic patterns, motif transformations, multi-track arrangement, orchestration rules, MIDI/MusicXML/PDF export, instrument-specific ranges, and model-backed generation with human review.
