# RiverSpendStream

RiverSpendStream is the streaming layer of RiverSpend TV.

## Target surfaces
- Web
- Smartphone
- Tablet
- Android TV / Google TV as a dedicated future app
- Other Smart TV platforms through dedicated applications and store approvals

## Content
- Films
- Music videos
- Series
- Creator content
- Concerts
- Live streaming
- Documentaries
- RiverSpend Originals

## Commerce
Content may link to authorized RiverSpendShop products such as CDs, albums, DVDs/Blu-ray, merchandise and tickets.

## Monetization
- Free/ad-supported content
- Paid promotion through RS Spons
- Future subscriptions
- Rental/purchase where rights allow
- Commerce commissions
- Artist/label royalty reporting

## Rule
Promotion does not buy fake views. Paid tiers increase eligible exposure/prioritization; views and engagement remain real metrics.

## Technical roadmap
1. Web content hub
2. Authenticated catalog and playback metadata
3. Secure video storage/transcoding/CDN
4. Rights and territory checks
5. Playback analytics
6. Commerce links
7. Android/Google TV application
8. Additional Smart TV applications
9. Live streaming provider integration
