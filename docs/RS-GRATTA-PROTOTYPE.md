# RS GRATTA! — Prototype and launch checklist

## User experience
- Homepage banner concept: RS GRATTA! — Acquista, scopri, raccogli i tuoi premi.
- Proposed reward eligibility: 1 free scratch ticket per eligible completed purchase; optional 2 tickets only under clearly disclosed promotional conditions.
- Demo page route: /rs-gratta
- This prototype is local-only and does not award actual rewards.

## Reward catalogue (illustrative, not configured)
- Shop vouchers
- Promotional RiverMoney (non-transferable and non-cashable; terms to be reviewed)
- Discounts
- Products/surprise rewards
- No-prize outcomes, if legally permissible and clearly disclosed

## Before launch
1. Italian counsel review; confirm whether and how DPR 430/2001 and related rules apply.
2. Draft official rules, eligibility, dates, odds/prize inventory and prize delivery.
3. Budget approval, inventory, prize liability and accounting/tax review.
4. Server-side eligibility tied to paid/settled orders, cancellation/return handling, duplicate prevention and audit trail.
5. Secure randomness and reward issuance on server only; never award from client-side randomness.
6. Privacy notice, abuse prevention, customer support and age/accessibility considerations.
7. Homepage banner insertion and order-system integration only after approval.

## Safety / integrity
- No paid ticket purchases or separate charges.
- No rewards can be purchased by retrying or spending additional money.
- Demo is clearly labeled and creates no real entitlements.
